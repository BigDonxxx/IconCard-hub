Four steps

From request to card.

1

Request a tier

Tell us the level, the kind of artist you follow, and where you are. This is an application, not an instant charge.

2

We confirm what is actually available

A coordinator replies with the live partner roster, the benefits that partner has agreed to, and a payment link only after that is clear.

3

Membership opens

Digital access starts first: the letter, the member area, and any content the partner has supplied.

4

The card follows

Physical cards for Gold and above are produced after payment clears. Signed cards wait on the partner’s signing window.
