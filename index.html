What the club is

A card, a tier, and a reason to stay.

A real membership

Five levels, from digital access to a tailored experience. Each tier has a published price and a written list of benefits, so a fan knows what they are buying.

A card worth keeping

The IconCard is the object at the center of the club: a member credential for events, drops, and partner spaces, not just a graphic on a landing page.

A share that gives back

A stated portion of membership fees is reserved for causes chosen with the partner, not buried in a footer sentence.

Built for a global fan

One club, several rooms.

The original site put the whole offer on one page. This version splits it the way a member would actually use it: choose a tier, understand the card, see how joining works, then request access.

Partner names are not listed here. The earlier page named film, music, and technology figures as if they were already attached to the club. Those names should only appear after a written partnership. Until then, the site talks about categories: film, music, sport, and global entertainment.
