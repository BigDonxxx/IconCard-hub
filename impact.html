The give-back

A share of the fee, named in advance.

The original page said a portion of proceeds would support causes chosen by the celebrities. That only works if the portion and the cause are written down before a fan pays.

Suggested split

Hold 10% of each collected membership fee for the partner’s named cause. Publish the cause, the amount reserved, and the date it was sent. Change the percentage only in the membership terms.

What not to promise yet

Do not name a hospital, school, or foundation until there is an agreement to receive the funds. A blank “charitable impact” line is more honest than a borrowed logo.
